# yadiggg context

`yadiggg` is the current product direction: an intended pocket field recorder and crate-digging assistant. **Crate IQ** is its earlier iteration; archived Crate IQ documents are provenance, not active requirements or approved hardware decisions.

## Terms

- **Design intent:** product or appearance direction that has not been translated into validated engineering.
- **Prototype:** exploratory code or a structural scaffold that may not run on the intended hardware.
- **Validated evidence:** revision-linked tests, measurements, and review records for actual artifacts.
- **Released for manufacture:** a controlled design revision with required evidence and approvals complete.

Use these distinctions when describing the product and repository. The proposed enclosure envelope, display, camera, control, battery, electronics, and connectors are unresolved or provisional; see the [product specification](physical-design/final-product-source-of-truth.md).
