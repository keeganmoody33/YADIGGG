# yadiggg Logo Refinement Round 1

These are exploration notes only. No logo route, vector artwork, or production identity is approved.

## Purpose

This document records the first controlled logo refinement pass after committing the visual-consistency and placement-sketch work.

## Source Direction

The logo work should refine the hand sketch concept, not the deprecated generic spade/record mark.

Core idea:

> A shovel-speaker mark: a shovel digging into vinyl/ground, where the shovel head also reads as a red speaker cone and emits green sound/light-wave energy.

## Generated Routes

### Route 1: Hardware Badge Mark

File: `../assets/brand/reference/img-mujspg9r-88019fbf.png`

Observed result:

- Clean dark background.
- Cream/white shovel silhouette.
- Red speaker-like cone/head.
- Green soundwave arcs.
- Lowercase `yadiggg` wordmark.
- Strongest small-hardware/badge readability of the three.

Strengths:

- Best balance between the rough sketch and usable logo system.
- Reads as a shovel-speaker hybrid more clearly than the polished orange prior mark.
- Good candidate for device boot screen, packaging mark, and small badge refinement.

Weaknesses:

- Shovel head still risks reading as an abstract megaphone/cone unless refined carefully.
- Wordmark dot/accent over the `i` is orange but may not need that detail.
- Needs one-color test and simplified etch version.

Recommendation: **Primary route to refine.**

### Route 2: Underground Signal Mark

File: `../assets/brand/reference/img-mujspi8y-83e51195.png`

Observed result:

- More expressive, poster-like shovel/speaker mark.
- Strong orange handle and large green arcs.
- Red cone/head is visible.
- Wordmark is brush-like orange/yellow.

Strengths:

- Preserves the rough energy from the source sketch.
- Good for stickers, launch graphics, posters, or social content.
- Feels more underground and physical than Route 3.

Weaknesses:

- Too complex and energetic for tiny hardware use.
- Wordmark is less disciplined and may become dated.
- More illustration than core identity system.

Recommendation: Keep as **expressive secondary/marketing route**, not primary hardware logo.

### Route 3: Technical Wordmark Lockup

File: `../assets/brand/reference/img-mujspgo1-16f6e9df.png`

Observed result:

- Cream background.
- Minimal black wordmark.
- Small icon with shovel, speaker-dot, and wave arcs.
- Very legible and restrained.

Strengths:

- Most suitable for documentation and small-screen use.
- Cleanest wordmark discipline.
- Easier to convert into vector and monochrome variants.

Weaknesses:

- Loses too much of the raw sketch energy.
- Shovel-speaker metaphor is smaller and more conventional.
- May feel more tech-logo than crate-digging hardware brand.

Recommendation: Use as **wordmark/system reference**, but inject more Route 1 character.

## User Direction

The user now prefers the **combined expressive shovel-speaker icon + technical wordmark direction**.

Earlier notes reported the following as a preferred direction:

- User direction: simplify the speaker interior / use less speaker detail.
- Composition: expressive Route 2-style shovel/speaker energy with Route 3-style clean technical `yadiggg` wordmark, now with a simpler red/orange speaker-cone shape for better small-size readability.

The referenced `img-muvo7mgq-aeae663b.png` file is not present in the tracked assets and has not been replaced with another image.

## Recommended Direction

Refine the **combined direction** as the primary logo route. Preserve the expressive oversized shovel-speaker head, orange shovel handle, red speaker-cone interior, green upward-right wave energy, and clean technical `yadiggg` wordmark.

Next refinements should not restart the logo system. They should only tune the icon geometry: make the shovel head less shield-like, clarify the open speaker mouth, and preserve waves traveling up/right from the speaker cone.

## Next Refinement Prompt

Generate a second round based on Route 1 with these constraints:

- Keep the Route 1 dark-background hardware badge feel.
- Make the shovel/speaker head more legible as both shovel and speaker.
- Reduce the green wave arcs to 2 or 3 clean arcs.
- Make the wordmark more like Route 3: geometric, compact, lowercase, high legibility.
- Create a one-color version candidate in the same sheet.
- Avoid generic spade/turntable mark, Crate IQ text, messy sketch texture, 3D effects, gradients, and tiny details.

## Acceptance Checks for Round 2

- Reads as a shovel at small size.
- Reveals red speaker cone at medium/large size.
- Keeps green soundwave energy without clutter.
- Wordmark says `yadiggg` clearly.
- Works in full color and one-color.
- Could be etched/printed small on the compact device.
- Does not look like the deprecated generic spade/record logo.
