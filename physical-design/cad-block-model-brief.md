[`cad/yadiggg_v1_block_model.scad`](cad/yadiggg_v1_block_model.scad) is a provisional block model for visualization only. It has not been reconciled against selected parts, measured enclosure geometry, internal fit, materials, tolerances, or assembly interfaces.

The previous modeling brief and its placeholder dimensions are preserved in [`archive/historical-proposals/cad-block-model-brief.md`](../archive/historical-proposals/cad-block-model-brief.md). Neither the brief nor the model is a manufacturing drawing or a fabrication-ready deliverable.
