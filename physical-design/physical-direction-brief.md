# Physical direction brief

The current physical direction is defined in the [product specification](final-product-source-of-truth.md). It is design intent only: the envelope is provisional, and display, camera, control mechanism, connectors, microphones, battery, electronics, materials, and geometry remain unresolved.

There is no approved placement sketch or enclosure design. The earlier placement and physical-direction exercises are preserved under `archive/historical-proposals/` for provenance; they contain unverified image observations and placeholder values. See the [readiness register](../docs/project-state.md) for what must be measured and reviewed before mechanical release.
