# yadiggg Logo Refinement Brief

This remains a logo exploration brief, not an approved identity package. The current logo image is concept art; the vector, one-color, etch-safe, and production artwork remain open.

## Purpose

## Core Logo Metaphor

The refined logo should express:

> A shovel-speaker mark: a shovel digging into vinyl/ground, where the shovel head also reads as a red speaker cone and emits green sound/light-wave energy.

It should work at two distances:

1. **Small / far away**: reads as a shovel digging downward.
2. **Large / close up**: reveals the speaker-cone head and soundwave/energy arcs.

## What Must Be Preserved

- `yadiggg` as the wordmark, not `Crate IQ`.
- Shovel/digging metaphor.
- Audio/speaker metaphor.
- Vinyl/record/crate-digging culture.
- Raw underground energy, but cleaned into a usable hardware brand mark.
- Visual compatibility with the current concept: smoke-clear appearance, dark internals, amber side-control appearance, and monochrome display concept.

## What Must Change

- The rough sketch must become a scalable vector-like mark.
- The shovel must not read as a generic spade or random microphone stand.
- The polished orange logo should not remain the final direction because it loses the red speaker cone and neon wave energy from the sketch.
- The logo should not be merch-first or oversized on the hardware.
- The wordmark should be cleaner and more controlled than the handwritten `.xyz` sketch.

## Three Refinement Routes

### Route 1: Hardware Badge Mark

A compact geometric emblem designed for small etched or printed use on the device, packaging, and startup screen. Minimal details, strong silhouette, shovel head simplified into a speaker cone, 2–3 energy arcs.

Best for: immediate hardware consistency.

### Route 2: Underground Signal Mark

A more expressive mark that preserves more of the original sketch energy: diagonal shovel, red speaker cone, green sonic arcs, rough-but-controlled line rhythm.

Best for: brand personality, stickers, web, launch visuals.

### Route 3: Technical Wordmark Lockup

A clean `yadiggg` wordmark paired with a reduced shovel-speaker icon. More geometric, less illustrative, better for tiny screen, documentation, and engraved applications.

Best for: production identity system and small-scale legibility.

## Acceptance Criteria

A refined logo passes only if:

- It is original and does not resemble an existing brand logo.
- It reads as `yadiggg` when text is included.
- It avoids `Crate IQ` completely.
- It does not become a generic spade/turntable icon.
- The shovel and speaker idea are both legible.
- The mark still works in one-color form.
- The full-color form preserves red speaker focal point and green wave energy.
- It can scale down for hardware use without becoming visual noise.
- It does not conflict with the compact smoke-clear product direction.

## Prompt Direction

Use the hand sketch as conceptual source, not as a literal trace. Use the existing polished orange logo only as a warning/reference for what became too generic.

New logo explorations should be original, commercially safe, vector-like, and suitable for later manual refinement in SVG/vector tools.
