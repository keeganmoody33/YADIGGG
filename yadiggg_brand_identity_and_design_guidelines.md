# yadiggg: Brand Identity and Design Guidelines
**Date of Document**: Wednesday, June 3, 2026

## 1. Executive Summary

This document establishes the official brand identity and design guidelines for **yadiggg**.

**yadiggg** combines raw underground crate-digging culture with premium, compact modern industrial design. The visual identity takes the organic composition of a personal notebook sketch and translates it into a precise hardware-first mark: a shovel digging into a record or ground surface, with the shovel head doubling as a red-highlighted speaker cone and neon light/soundwave arcs blowing back from it. It is a visual system engineered for a small, second-pocket audio hardware tool, blending street-level authenticity with professional, clean product aesthetics.

---

## 2. The Core Brand Identity & Logo Mark

### 2.1 The Visual Metaphor: "The Shovel Speaker Dig"
The official **yadiggg** logo is based on the source sketch in `yadigggsketch.pdf` / `yadigggsketch_rendered.png`. It is a precise geometric mark that must work at two reading distances:

1. **Zoomed out**: the silhouette reads as a shovel digging downward into a record or ground surface.
2. **Close up**: the shovel head reveals itself as a red-highlighted speaker cone, with neon light/soundwave arcs blowing backward from the speaker.

```
             HANDLE / SHAFT
                  |
                  |
                  |
              ____|____
             /         \
            /  SPEAKER  \     )))  )))
           /  RED CONE   \   NEON SOUNDWAVES
          /_______________\
                 \
                  \___ digging into record / ground line
```

*   **The Shovel Silhouette**: The handle and shaft create the primary "digging" read. The mark should still feel like a shovel when seen small or from a distance.
*   **The Red Speaker Head**: The shovel head is not a generic spade. It contains the speaker form, with the speaker cone highlighted in red as the emotional and sonic focal point.
*   **The Neon Soundwave Arcs**: Soundwaves/light arcs blow back from the speaker head, creating motion, volume, and the sense that the record is physically producing sound.
*   **The Record/Ground Cut**: The lower line or contact point should imply the shovel digging into a vinyl record, groove surface, or ground plane.
*   **The ".diggg" Wordmark**: When used, it should stay secondary to the shovel-speaker mark and use a compact geometric sans-serif treatment.

## 4. Logo Variations & Usage Guidelines

For maximum flexibility across diverse media types (screen, silk-screen, laser-engraving, emboss), the logo is verified in three official variations:

```
  +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
  |                           LOGO VARIATIONS                          |
  +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
  |                                                                    |
  |  [ VARIATION 1 ]         [ VARIATION 2 ]        [ VARIATION 3 ]    |
  |  Amber Orange on         Flat White on          Solid Black on     |
  |  Charcoal Base           Matte Black            Cream White Base   |
  |  (Default Brand Vibe)    (Technical Screen)     (Merch & Print)    |
  |                                                                    |
  +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 4.1 Master Usage Rules
1.  **Two-distance readability**: At small size, the mark must read as a shovel digging downward; at larger size, the red speaker cone and neon soundwave arcs must become visible.
2.  **Clear Space**: Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to 20% of total logo height to prevent visual crowding.
3.  **Minimum Size**: 
    *   *Print / Etch*: The logo should never be produced smaller than **12mm x 12mm** if the red speaker and neon wave details must remain visible.
    *   *Digital/Screen*: On the 2.7" Sharp Memory LCD screen, the logo should never be rendered smaller than **50px x 50px**.
4.  **Prohibited Uses**:
    *   Do NOT simplify the mark into a generic spade.
    *   Do NOT remove the red speaker focal point when the logo is shown in color.
    *   Do NOT remove the neon soundwave/light arcs from full-color logo applications.
    *   Do NOT stretch, skew, or alter the vector proportions of the shovel-speaker silhouette.
    *   Do NOT let packaging, tote bags, or merch mockups define the product identity before the compact hardware direction is resolved.

## 5. Physical Brand Applications (Hardware-First)

The brand identity of **yadiggg** is hardware-first. The priority is not a packaging ecosystem, tote bag, or merch system; the priority is a compact dedicated tool that can live in a second pocket without feeling like a phone or bulky walkie-talkie.

### 5.1 Compact Device Body
*   **Scale**: Smaller than a phone; closer to a slim pocket audio recorder or lighter-sized dedicated music tool.
*   **Material**: Dark smoke-grey translucent polycarbonate shell.
*   **Control Accent**: Small horizontal amber-orange side button for scan/capture.
*   **Visual Role**: The body should look quiet, dense, and practical, with subtle visible internals rather than oversized gadget styling.

### 5.2 Logo Placement on Hardware
*   **Primary Placement**: Small etched, printed, or molded mark on the device back or sleeve-facing side.
*   **Logo Read**: From distance, the mark reads as the shovel digging downward. Up close, the red speaker cone and neon soundwave arcs become visible.
*   **Constraint**: Do not use generic spade artwork or merch-first logo treatments as the reference for the hardware identity.

### 5.3 Deferred Applications
*   **Packaging, tote bags, and merch** are intentionally deferred. They should not drive the visual language until the compact hardware form and corrected shovel-speaker logo are finalized.
